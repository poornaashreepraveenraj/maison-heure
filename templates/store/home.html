{# 6. Authentication process #}
{% include "partials/section_head.html" with eyebrow="Our process" title="Four steps to certified" sub="No watch is listed until it has passed every stage." heading_id="process-title" %}
01

Inspection

Case, dial, hands, serials and bracelet are examined under magnification against reference data.

02

Movement check

We open the caseback and measure rate, amplitude and beat error on a timegrapher.

03

Polishing

Only on request — most collectors prefer honest, unpolished cases, and so do we.

04

Certified

A signed report, photographs and our 12-month warranty travel with every watch.
