About Maison Heure

Every watch, verified by hand

We are a small team of watchmakers and collectors giving fine watches a second life.

Why pre-owned?

A well-made mechanical watch can run for generations. Buying pre-owned means less waste, access to discontinued references and fair prices — as long as you can trust what you buy.

That trust is our product. Every watch we list has been inspected in our workshop, regulated if needed, photographed honestly and documented with a signed report.

{% include "partials/section_head.html" with eyebrow="Authentication" title="The four steps" %}
01

Inspection

Serial and reference numbers, dial printing, hands, crystal, case finishing and bracelet are checked under 10× magnification.

02

Movement check

The caseback is opened in a dust-free bench; we confirm the calibre and measure rate, amplitude and beat error.

03

Polishing

Optional and conservative. Original, unpolished cases keep more value, so we only refinish on request.

04

Certified

A signed certificate, timegrapher printout and photographs are issued, and the 12-month warranty begins on delivery.

Questions about a specific piece?
